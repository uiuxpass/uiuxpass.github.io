Changelog

Every update is free for existing customers.

2.4.1 · 24 November 2026

A sixth kit, the AI Output Eval Kit: four new skills and four new scripts, 45 of each in all.

  • ai-output-rubric (new): define what a good output from an AI agent looks like before judging it: 4 to 12 criteria anyone can score the same way, with pass and fail examples, severity and who checks each one; packs for answers, designs, HTML pages, documents and agent actions; a test set with edge and risky cases; a release gate; a linter that flags vague criteria.
  • ai-output-review (new): is one AI answer, generated screen, HTML page, document or agent action good enough for the person who receives it? A script counts what can be counted (answer first, filler, long sentences, buzzwords, numbers without a source, overclaims, placeholders, endings that hand the work back, unsafe requests; the 66-check polish checker for HTML), then the rest of the rubric is scored with evidence.
  • ai-output-eval (new): score many outputs and compare prompts, models or versions on the same cases: how many cases you need, a blind side-by-side page, pass rates with 95% ranges, cases fixed and broken, an exact paired test, a release gate and a report page.
  • ai-judge-calibration (new): can you trust an AI judge? Agreement, kappa and bias against your reviewers for each criterion, whether the judge agrees with itself on a second run, whether swapping the order changes the winner, and a verdict per criterion: trust, spot-check or score by hand.
  • Install: steps follow the new Plugins page in Claude (type / in any chat to call a skill). Team and Enterprise: Skills must stay on in admin settings, or plugins stop working from November 30, 2026.
  • figma-build: reuses a file's library variables and component slots before creating new ones, and explains what to do when a Figma admin must approve the connector.

2.4.0 · 23 November 2026

One new skill, 41 in all, and three new scripts, 41 in all.

  • sales-page-trust (new): would a stranger pay on this page? 12 checks a careful buyer makes, measured in a real browser: price and what you get, a working buy link to a known checkout, terms and refund policy, a real maker, a way to reach them, real product proof, the same number saying the same thing everywhere, sourced claims, checkable testimonials, countdowns reloaded as a new visitor to catch fake urgency, an FAQ that answers the usual worries, signs the page is current.
  • interface-polish: an 8-sign check for pages that look built from a template or by AI (reported next to the 66, never added to it) and a side-by-side comparison of your first screen with two best-in-class pages you pick.
  • Tested: on this site's own earlier versions and on public pages. It caught our earlier home page, which passed 66 of 66 yet looked like a template, and a before-score that said 38 in one place and 39 in another.
  • Design system kit: the full-kit zip again includes three ready-made design systems (Ledger, Sprout, Ember), rebuilt with design-system-starter: all 22 contrast pairs pass AA and each components page passes 66 of 66.
