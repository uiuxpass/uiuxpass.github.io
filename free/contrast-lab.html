    What contrast is

    Watch out

    The maths, live

    text #767676 L = 0.1812
    bg #FFFFFF L = 1.0000
    L = 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B
    ratio = (1.0000 + 0.05) / (0.1812 + 0.05)
    = 4.54 : 1
    body text needs ≥ 4.5 · large text ≥ 3
