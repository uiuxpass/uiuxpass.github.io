Is your AI-made page actually finished?

Paste the HTML of a page Claude, v0, Lovable or Cursor made. In a few seconds you get 16 measured checks at desktop and phone width, each with how to fix it.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored. Scripts in your page are not run.

What it checks

Contrast, text size, tap targets at 390 px, sideways scrolling on phones, blocked zoom, fake buttons, unlabeled fields, missing alt text, heading order, removed focus outlines, motion without a reduced-motion rule, emoji used as icons, purple-gradient and gradient-text "AI look", cards inside cards, and unfinished filler copy. These are 16 of the 66 checks in UI/UX Pass's interface-polish skill, which also walks the page with a real keyboard, measures layout shift and fixes what it finds.
