Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This website

This site has no ads and sets no tracking cookies. To see which parts of the page help and which confuse people, it uses Microsoft Clarity with cookies turned off: it records page views, scrolling and clicks, and masks text you type. Microsoft processes this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Gumroad. We receive your name, email address, country and what you bought, so we can deliver updates and support. We never see your card details. See Gumroad's privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

The skills

UI/UX Pass runs inside your own Claude account. We do not receive your prompts, files or results. The checker scripts run in Claude's environment or on your computer and send nothing to us. The same applies to the two free skills (also listed in the Claude directory): they make no network requests and store nothing.

Email

We email you only about your purchase and product updates. You can ask us to delete your data at any time: support@uiuxpass.com.
